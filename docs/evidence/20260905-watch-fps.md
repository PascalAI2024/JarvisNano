# The six watches, measured — 2026-09-05, wave N13 image

Method: over Wi-Fi, walk the styles with synthetic swipes on WATCH, and per
style take two samples of `/api/diag/tasks` and `/api/display` four seconds
apart. `gfx_render ms/frame` is that task's run-time delta divided by the
flush strips delta / 39; `render_frame_us` is the new render clock's own
figure for the strip loop (the overlay only). `actual_fps` is the cockpit's.

Reading: the ≥ 17 fps gate (PLAN N12.6 / N13.13) is not met on any style.
The overlay is 17–61 ms of a 51–92 ms frame; JARVIS, which has no dial,
still costs 54 ms a frame at 240 MHz and 67 at 160 — the engine's per-strip
decode and QSPI flush do not scale with the clock, so the CPU levers on the
overlay could not reach the gate. A muted device never leaves the rest gear
even with a session open (the quiet ladder), which is why the live-gear run
needed the owner's hand.
